## 🔭 What I'm working on

Three open-source Chrome extensions that embed an AI assistant directly into the enterprise tools you already use. Each one connects to your logged-in account and lets you make changes in plain natural language instead of navigating menus and forms.

| Project | Platform | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **ServiceNow AI Extension** | ServiceNow | 🚧 Open-sourcing soon |
| **Jira AI Extension** | Jira | 🚧 Open-sourcing soon |
| **Outlook AI Extension** | Outlook | 🚧 Open-sourcing soon |

**The idea:** instead of manually updating tickets, triaging incidents, or managing your inbox, you talk to an AI that acts on your behalf — with your permissions, in your session, with a clear security model for what it can and can't touch.
